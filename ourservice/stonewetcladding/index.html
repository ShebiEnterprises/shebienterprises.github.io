What is it?

Stone wet cladding

Stone wet cladding uses a mortar-based adhesive system to fix natural stone tiles directly onto building surfaces. It is the traditional and most durable method of stone application for exterior walls, building facades, feature walls and podium areas.

We engineer movement joints, waterproofing interfaces and drip details so stone performs through monsoon cycles.

Capabilities

  • Podium and plinth stone with drainage layers
  • Interior feature walls and lift lobbies
  • Colour-matched grout and sealant systems
  • Coordination with waterproofing and RCC tolerances

FAQ

Wet cladding bonds stone with mortar; dry cladding uses mechanical anchors without mortar, preferred for many high-rises.

Granite and quartzites resist salt and humidity well; porous stones need stronger sealers and more frequent maintenance.

With correct detailing and maintenance, wet-fixed granite commonly exceeds decades of service life.

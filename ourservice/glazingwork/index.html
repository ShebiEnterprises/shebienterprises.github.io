What is it?

Glazing works

Glazing works cover curtain wall and all major glass envelope types - stick, unitised and semi-structural - plus partitions and decorative applications. Shebi Enterprises handles the full spectrum from design assist to site sealing.

We coordinate with aluminium system vendors for gaskets, pressure plates, thermal breaks and drainage to meet consultant performance briefs, including projects on green rating pathways where specified.

Capabilities

  • Curtain wall, stick and unitised site glazing
  • Semi-unitised and semi-structural envelope interfaces
  • Performance mock-ups and water testing support
  • Spandrel and vision glass coordination
  • Atrium and skylight interfaces

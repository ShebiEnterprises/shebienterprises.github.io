Facade Survey & Leak Diagnosis

Photo-documented facade surveys and leak diagnosis that feed repair scopes.

What is it?

Facade Survey & Leak Diagnosis

Guessing the leak source wastes silicone and scaffold money. Pattern mapping against wind direction, joint lines and interfaces finds whether you have sealant failure, blocked weeps or condensation.

Our surveys produce actionable notes for societies, PMs and insurers - then feed maintenance or retrofit packages.

What the client needs

A complete pack produces a buildable estimate and fewer site claims. Share what you have via contact - we will list gaps plainly.

  • Building access permissions
  • Known wet patch locations
  • As-builts if available
  • Preferred access method
  • Reporting format needs

Capabilities

  • Visual and photo surveys
  • Leak pattern mapping
  • Hose-pack retests after repair
  • BMU / rope access surveys
  • Repair scope recommendations

HPL & Solid Aluminium Cladding

HPL and solid aluminium panel systems when the brief needs different fire or durability behaviour than ACP.

What is it?

HPL & Solid Aluminium Cladding

HPL and solid aluminium are specified when architects want different edge detailing, impact resistance or fire assemblies than standard ACP. They are heavier and need stronger subframes, but can be the right answer for feature bands and high-abuse zones.

We help teams compare options against ACP and install to approved systems.

What the client needs

A complete pack produces a buildable estimate and fewer site claims. Share what you have via contact - we will list gaps plainly.

  • Panel brand and thickness
  • Fire assembly certificates
  • Subframe capacity check
  • Joint and edge detailing
  • Sample approval under site light

Capabilities

  • HPL rainscreen install
  • Solid aluminium cassette systems
  • Subframe upgrades
  • Transitions to ACP and stone
  • Sample and mock-up walls

Unitised Curtain Wall

Factory-sealed unitised modules for repetitive towers with Mumbai logistics planning.

What is it?

Unitised Curtain Wall

Unitised curtain wall improves quality control by sealing modules in the factory and hanging them on site. Crane windows, trailer access and staging yards decide whether oversized modules are realistic in dense Mumbai plots.

We coordinate fabrication partners, glass procurement and site hang sequences with clear interface ownership.

What the client needs

A complete pack produces a buildable estimate and fewer site claims. Share what you have via contact - we will list gaps plainly.

  • Module grid and elevations
  • Factory slot and glass lead times
  • Crane / trailer access study
  • Anchor and embed survey
  • Stick vs unitised podium decision

Capabilities

  • Unitised hang programmes
  • Hybrid stick podium + unitised tower
  • Logistics planning
  • Interface detailing
  • Site QA on hang days

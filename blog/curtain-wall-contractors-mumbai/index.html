Curtain Wall Contractors in Mumbai: Scope Checklist

Scope checklist for hiring curtain wall contractors in Mumbai - stick vs unitised, testing, interfaces, logistics and documentation by Shebi Enterprises.

Article facts

  • Publisher: Shebi Enterprises, Mumbai
  • Topic: Curtain Wall Contractors in Mumbai: Scope Checklist
  • Audience: developers, architects and facade consultants
  • Contact for project advice: info@shebienterprises.com

Hiring curtain wall contractors in Mumbai requires a clear scope - not only glass and aluminium. Interfaces, logistics and testing decide whether the envelope stays dry through monsoon. Use this checklist when comparing bids for glazing works.

System selection

Confirm stick, semi-unitised or unitised early. For structural silicone options, compare with our notes on structural vs semi-unitised glazing and structural glass services.

Scope checklist

  • Design assist vs build-to-print responsibility
  • Glass build-up (IGU, coatings, spandrel) and thermal targets
  • Anchor design to RCC / steel and survey tolerances
  • Fire stopping, smoke seals and slab-edge details
  • ACP / stone / canopy interfaces with nominated parties
  • Scaffold, BMU, crane and night-work assumptions
  • Mock-up size, location and acceptance criteria
  • Warranty language for glass, silicone and finish

Mumbai logistics realities

Unitised modules need staging space and crane windows that dense plots may not allow every day. Stick systems need more site labour but can adapt to irregular podiums. Ask bidders to show a hoist calendar, not only a Gantt bar.

Documentation you should demand

Shop drawings, glass schedules, silicone data sheets, as-built anchor maps and maintenance access notes. Without these, facade maintenance becomes guesswork after handover.

FAQ

Unitised often wins on tall repeat grids with factory quality control; stick can suit podiums, renovations and irregular geometry.

Water and air infiltration, structural mock-up performance where specified, and sealant adhesion checks on site samples.

Clarify in tender. Ambiguous interfaces are the most common source of leaks and claims.

## Content rules (every new post)

1. **Length:** 1,200–1,800 words (guides) or 900–1,200 (comparisons/checklists).
2. **H1:** one clear keyword phrase + Mumbai/India where natural.
3. **Structure:** intro answer → sections with H2/H3 → FAQ (3–5) → CTA.
4. **Internal links (minimum):**
   - 1–2 relevant **service** pages
   - 1 related **blog** post
   - 1 **portfolio/project** where relevant
   - 1 strong CTA to **`/contact/`**
5. **Schema:** `BlogPosting` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` + unique OG image when available.
6. **Image:** dedicated hero 1600×900 + descriptive alt; fall back to `og-default.jpg` for social if needed.
7. **E-E-A-T:** mention Shebi Enterprises site practice, mock-ups, Mumbai climate, without fake awards.
8. **Slug:** short kebab-case, keyword-first.

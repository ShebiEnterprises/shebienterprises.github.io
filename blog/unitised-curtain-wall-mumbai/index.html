Why unitised wins on repetitive towers

Factory-sealed modules improve quality control and speed once crane windows open. Compare options in structural vs semi-unitised.

Mumbai logistics constraints

Staging yards, trailer access and night crane permits decide whether oversized modules are realistic. Dense plots may need smaller module grids even if aesthetics prefer larger glass.

Fabrication and tolerances

Anchor brackets and embedding plates must match survey. Our fabrication and glazing teams coordinate shim strategies before hang days.

When stick still competes

Irregular podiums, renovations and late design changes often favour stick systems. See when stick glazing still makes sense.

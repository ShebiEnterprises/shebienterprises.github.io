Key services: Stonedrycladding.

What anchors do

Mechanical fixings transfer stone weight and wind loads into the subframe or structure for dry cladding.

Kerf quality matters

Poor kerfs crack edges under load. Factory cutting tolerances and stainless grades suit coastal Mumbai air.

Open joints vs sealed

Open-joint rainscreens need designed cavity drainage—see open joint stone facades.

Inspection

Annual checks of fasteners and sealant at penetrations protect the system long term.

FAQ

Structural Glazing vs Semi-Unitised Systems

Compare structural silicone glazing and semi-unitised curtain wall systems for Mumbai projects - aesthetics, testing, programme and maintenance implications.

Article facts

  • Publisher: Shebi Enterprises, Mumbai
  • Topic: Structural Glazing vs Semi-Unitised Systems
  • Audience: developers, architects and facade consultants
  • Contact for project advice: info@shebienterprises.com

Architects often debate structural glazing vs semi-unitised systems for Mumbai towers. Both can perform well - the right choice depends on aesthetics, factory capacity, site access and maintenance strategy. Shebi Enterprises installs and coordinates both through our structural glass and glazing teams.

Structural glazing in brief

Glass is bonded with structural silicone to a carrier frame or fin system, reducing visible exterior mullions. Premium lobbies and feature walls use this for a seamless look. Quality hinges on compatible silicone, clean substrates, controlled curing and adhesion testing. See also structural glazing in Mumbai.

Semi-unitised / unitised in brief

Factory-assembled modules with aluminium frames arrive ready to hang. Weather seals and gaskets are more systemised. This suits repetitive commercial grids and reduces on-site silicone dependency for primary weather lines.

Decision factors

  • Aesthetics: structural for minimal metal; unitised for crisp framed grids
  • Programme: unitised favours parallel factory work; structural needs careful site sequencing
  • Access: large modules need crane strategy; structural may use smaller glass packs
  • Maintenance: plan replacement routes for both - bonded glass swaps differ from unit swaps

Hybrid tip

Many Mumbai offices use structural or spider glazing at the lobby and unitised curtain wall above. Define the interface early so waterproofing and fire stopping do not become change orders.

FAQ

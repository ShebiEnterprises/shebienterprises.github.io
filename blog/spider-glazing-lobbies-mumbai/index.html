Spider Glazing for Lobbies and Atriums in Mumbai

How spider glazing works for Mumbai lobbies and atriums - fittings, glass specs, waterproofing at base and maintenance access by Shebi Enterprises.

Article facts

  • Publisher: Shebi Enterprises, Mumbai
  • Topic: Spider Glazing for Lobbies and Atriums in Mumbai
  • Audience: developers, architects and facade consultants
  • Contact for project advice: info@shebienterprises.com

Spider glazing gives Mumbai lobbies and atriums a transparent, jewel-like presence with point-fixed stainless fittings. Done well, it feels effortless; done poorly, it leaks at the base and shows every fabrication tolerance. Shebi Enterprises delivers these packages under spider glazing alongside structural glass feature walls.

Where spider glazing shines

Entrance lobbies, hotel drop-offs, office atriums and connecting bridges benefit from minimal framing. For system comparisons with framed envelopes, read structural vs semi-unitised and our broader structural glazing guide.

Engineering basics

  • Glass thickness and heat soak strategy for Mumbai wind exposure
  • Spider / rotule grade (typically SS 316 in coastal air)
  • Fin or cable support philosophy and deflection limits
  • Hole positions and edge distances from approved shop drawings

Waterproofing and base details

Most lobby leaks start at the sill, not the spider. Coordinate stone thresholds, drainage and sealant compatibility early. If the lobby connects to canopies, involve skylights and canopies detailing so load paths and flashings are not invented on site.

Maintenance access

Plan for cleaning and fitting inspection without damaging stone floors. Document torque and glass marks at handover for future maintenance teams.

Planning a lobby glass wall in Mumbai? Share lobby sections and glass sizes via our contact page for a buildable proposal.

FAQ

It is most common on lobbies, atriums and feature walls. Full-height towers usually combine it with curtain wall systems.
